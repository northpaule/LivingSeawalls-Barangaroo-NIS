# Living Seawalls @ Barangaroo
This project is part of the **Living Seawalls** project with the *Sydney Institute of Marine Science* (SIMS) and particularly aims to reduce the number of non-indigenous species (NIS) on panels through underwater gardening (hand removal) of two key ascidians NIS: *Botrylloides leachii* and *Styela plicata*, at Watermans Cove in Barangaroo, Sydney, NSW.

We acknowledge the Gadigal People of the Eora Nation as the Traditional Owners and Custodians of the land and waters on which this project takes place, and we pay our respects to their Elders past, present and emerging. We recognise their continuing connection to this land and  sea country that has sustained the Gadigal people for tens of thousands of years. We honour the cultural, spiritual, and ancestral significance of this place, and acknowledge that sovereignty was never ceded. We extend this respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.
